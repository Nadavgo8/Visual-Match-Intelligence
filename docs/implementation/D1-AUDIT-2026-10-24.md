still counted, although the t=0 shot was already outside the seven-minute window.

and inspect its published meters, without inserting a compensating cleanup tick.

Learning: a correct cleanup boundary is insufficient when readers publish between
cleanup calls. Check the emitted result immediately before/at/after expiry, with
no further cleanup event, for every sliding-window consumer.
