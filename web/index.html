Visual Match Intelligence

Football's sound, made visible. Whistles, the crowd and the commentary become calm visual signals on the screen and a companion app on the phone, for fans with hearing difficulty.

📺 TV viewThe broadcast: 2D replay with whistle banners, an atmosphere indicator and momentum. 📱 Companion appOpen on your phone: event feed, stoppage analysis with receipts, and Ask.
  • Every whistle type has its own banner in a fixed place. No captions to read during play.
  • Analysis waits for stoppages and comes at your depth: a friend explaining, or an analyst.
  • Every sentence carries its receipts: the event IDs it came from, checked by a validator.

All teams, players and matches are synthetic, produced by our own seeded match generator. No real footage, data, clubs or league marks are used. Raw data: events, crowd, squads.
